# SEO pulse, 2026-09

Against: none, this is the first pulse and the baseline. Written: 2026-09-21.

## Summary

The catalog is not yet found through search. Of 25 terms it can own, one appears in the top 20:
`internal agents map`, the site's own name, at position 10, and Google lists it under the legacy
`/definitions.html` name. Every subject term and every *organization plus system* term is held by
the primary source and by news sites. The site is technically sound: no critical or high defect,
no repeated head text, a sitemap that matches the published routes. The one template defect worth
fixing is on all 35 organization pages. The largest opportunity is not in this data: it is the two
Search Console exports this pulse did not have.

## Demand

45 tracked keywords, DataForSEO Google Ads planner data, United States. First snapshot, so there
is no month-over-month mover list; the 12-month trend gives the momentum below.

The catalog's own vocabulary sits at the reporting floor: `internal ai agents` 20, `companies
using ai agents` 20, `ai agent case studies` 10, and 15 terms return `null` (among them `ai coding
agents`, `code review agent`, `ai bug triage`, `ai on call`, `how companies use ai agents`). This
is below the advertiser threshold, not zero demand. It means the planner cannot see this subject
and Search Console must.

The large numbers are vendor head terms the catalog will not hold: `claude code` 550,000,
`agentic ai` 90,500, `mcp server` 60,500, `model context protocol` 12,100.

Momentum, last three months against the three before:

- Rising: `enterprise ai agents` 2,400, +1,474%, cost per click $88.75. Advertisers moved in;
  the catalog can serve the informational side of that query, not compete for the click.
- Rising: `agent evals` 260, +40%; `ai data analyst` 720, +53%.
- Falling: most of the space fell 25 to 50%, including `agentic ai` (−41%), `claude code` (−51%),
  `mcp server` (−38%), `ai agent examples` (−32%), `human in the loop ai` (−33%). One snapshot
  cannot separate summer seasonality from a flattening curve. Read again in October.

Terms with demand and no record: none found. The rising terms are concepts, not organizations.

## Drift and discovery

- The "companies using AI agents internally" results are held by lists that sell something:
  MagmaLabs (seven vendors' agent products, no internal agents), TechRev, tkxel, Aurigait.
  None of them answers the question the catalog answers. None links to the catalog.
- Department of Product (Substack) sells a paywalled "Deep Dive" of "over 50 different new
  examples of internal AI agents in practice from Stripe, GitHub, Notion, Replit, Atlassian and
  more". The framing and the organization list match the catalog's overview closely. It does not
  link to the catalog. This is a reader of the map, or a parallel effort, and a candidate for an
  outreach conversation rather than an SEO action.
- Notion Scruff and Stripe Minions each have a full secondary layer now (ByteByteGo, InfoQ,
  Lenny's Newsletter, MindStudio, a Hacker News thread). The catalog is absent from both. Its
  entry pages carry the one thing the secondary layer does not: claim-by-claim provenance. That
  is the argument for a link, not for more text.
- Keywords to add next month: `agent evals` is already tracked; add `ai agent evaluation
  harness`, `agent harness examples`, and the *organization plus system* terms
  `catalog-terms.mjs --untracked` lists for records added in September (39 of 56 records have no
  tracked term today).
