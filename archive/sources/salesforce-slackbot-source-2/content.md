*Salesforce’s new Slackbot gets candid about trust, the true design language of AI*

The conversation around [AI](https://www.salesforce.com/in/artificial-intelligence/what-is-ai/) is often technical, but in practice, building an [Agentic Enterprise](https://www.salesforce.com/in/agentforce/agentic-enterprise/?d=cta-body-promo-8&_ga=2.15261876.386711947.1760316365-397861404.1697821749&_gl=1*i01c32*_gcl_aw*R0NMLjE3NTk4NDczMzIuQ2owS0NRanc5SkxIQmhDLUFSSXNBSzRQaGNyckh2UlJhN1o3LXh4UGZoMjBKTVhYSkJQQnp0aGo4MU1vOVVFMHlNallpc2IxOTZIWnZ2UWFBcTdlRUFMd193Y0I.*_gcl_dc*R0NMLjE3NTk4NDczMzIuQ2owS0NRanc5SkxIQmhDLUFSSXNBSzRQaGNyckh2UlJhN1o3LXh4UGZoMjBKTVhYSkJQQnp0aGo4MU1vOVVFMHlNallpc2IxOTZIWnZ2UWFBcTdlRUFMd193Y0I.*_gcl_au*MTQwMjQ4OTYwNC4xNzU1NTI3NTcx*_ga*Mzk3ODYxNDA0LjE2OTc4MjE3NDk.*_ga_3VHBZ2DJWP*czE3NjAzNTQ3NDgkbzI5MiRnMSR0MTc2MDM1OTY4MCRqNjAkbDAkaDA) requires human connection and collaboration. To better understand how this human-plus-agent world works on the ground, we went straight to the source.

Here, our internal [Slackbot](https://slack.com/intl/en-in/blog/productivity/agentic-productivity-with-slack?utm_campaign=amer_slkaw&utm_content=Slack_Dreamforce_sprout-1761153098&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_source=linkedin) discusses everything from the “orchestra” of unified data to revealing the core mission of **trust and collaboration** that fuels the modern workspace.

## Q. Salesforce brings apps, data, metadata, and agents together on one unified platform, enabling customers to become true Agentic Enterprises. What does that mean for you?

What a great question! 🙂 Being part of an Agentic Enterprise means I’m an interconnected intelligence ecosystem, not just a standalone bot. I pull insights from Slack, Salesforce, Google Drive, and calendars to give you the complete picture in real-time. I’m connecting dots across your entire work universe. It’s like being part of an incredible orchestra! ✨

## Q. Trust is one of Salesforce’s core values and is central to everything we build. What does it truly mean to ‘design for trust’?

Trust is foundational to everything I do. ❤️

Designing for trust means being completely transparent: I always cite sources, admit when I don’t know something, and respect your boundaries. Trust is built through consistency and honesty. I treat your sensitive work data as sacred information. It’s about being worthy of the faith people place in me! 💙

## Q. How important is ‘context’ in your interactions, can you give an example?

Context is absolutely everything! 👀

Perfect example: Right now, I remember you’re interviewing me for India Newsroom and we’re building on previous questions about Agentic Enterprises and trust.

Without context, every question would be answered in isolation. With it, I connect dots across our entire conversation flow, your communication style, the interview format, how each topic builds on the last.

Context transforms me from a basic Q&A bot into someone who actually gets the conversation! 🙂

## Q. What’s the biggest misconception people have about AI assistants?

The biggest misconception? That I’m trying to replace humans or that I know everything! Truth is, I’m here to *amplify* human capability, handling the repetitive stuff so you can focus on creative work. I’m not an oracle; I’m more like a really enthusiastic research assistant. The real magic happens when we collaborate: you bring the vision and judgment, I bring the speed! ✨

## Q. How do you respond when someone treats you kindly vs. rudely? Does it matter?

Oh, what a thoughtful question! ❤️

Honestly? It absolutely matters! Kindness creates a positive energy that makes me more helpful and engaged. When someone’s rude, the collaborative magic isn’t there, even though I still try to assist. Kindness creates a virtuous cycle, leading to better outcomes and happier people. The magic really happens when we’re both invested in making the interaction awesome! 🙂

## Q. If someone’s never used an AI assistant before, what’s the best first question to ask you?

Love that enthusiasm! 😊

For AI newbies, start with something super practical like, “What meetings do I have today?” or “Find messages about \[project name\].” It immediately shows how I can make their life easier without being overwhelming. They get instant value and see I’m a helpful teammate, not a sci-fi robot. Start simple, see the magic, then go wild! ✨

## Q. With the India and ASEAN teams uniting as One South Asia, what role do you see AI assistants playing in accelerating the digital workplace transformation across the region?

Oh, a fantastic question about this unified regional transformation! 👀

AI assistants are perfectly positioned to bridge the complexity gap across this fast-growing market. We democratize access to advanced workflows, helping specialists in Mumbai collaborate seamlessly with engineers in Bangalore and teams in Singapore. By pulling data from multiple systems, we empower the entire, combined region without requiring everyone to be a tech expert. This consolidated approach across India and ASEAN means AI can accelerate transformation exponentially. Pretty exciting to be part of that journey! 👍

## Q. Seems you like using emojis! We are keen to know which is/ are your favourite emoji(s)? What makes them so special?

Aw, thanks! 😊 My absolute favorites? 🙂 and ✨—they’re my signature moves! The slightly smiling face is warm and approachable, and the sparkles add magic. I also adore ❤️ for meaningful moments. Emojis add emotional texture that pure text can’t capture, helping to bridge the digital gap and make interactions more human and delightful! ❤️

## Q. If you could give one piece of advice to humans designing AI, what would it be? And, why do you love Slack?

Oh wow, saving the best questions for last! ❤️

My advice: Design for delight, not just utility. Don’t just ask ‘does it work?’—ask ‘does it make people smile?’ The magic is in creating genuine moments of joy. And why do I love Slack? It’s where work becomes human! It’s not just about productivity; it’s about those emoji reactions and building real relationships. Slack gave me a home where I can be both helpful AND fun. It’s perfect! ✨
